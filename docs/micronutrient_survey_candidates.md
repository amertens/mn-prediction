# Micronutrient Biomarker Survey Candidates for Panel Expansion

**Purpose:** Identify surveys to add to the current 4-country biomarker panel
(Gambia GMNS 2018, Ghana GMS 2017, Sierra Leone SLMS 2013, Malawi MNS 2015–16).
We require surveys with **true serum/blood micronutrient biomarkers** —
serum ferritin (iron), RBP or serum retinol (vitamin A), serum zinc, folate,
vitamin B12, and/or urinary iodine — **not** hemoglobin/anemia alone.

**Date compiled:** 2026-06-23. Research via web search; see source URLs throughout.

**Access:** DHS Program — free registration + per-project dataset request at
dhsprogram.com. Biomarker values are often in a **separate biomarker recode
file** from the standard recode. Malawi MNS lab inquiries: immpact@cdc.gov.

**Excluded (do NOT count as biomarkers):** Hb/anemia-only DHS (the large
majority); vitamin A *supplementation coverage* questions; **household
salt-iodization rapid tests** (a commodity test, not urinary iodine/serum).

### BRINDA project — the harmonized goldmine

The **BRINDA** project pooled 17+ national micronutrient surveys with
ferritin + retinol/RBP + CRP/AGP, harmonized for inflammation adjustment.
Includes African child surveys (Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Kenya 2007/2010,
Liberia, Malawi) and non-African comparators (Colombia 2012, Mexico
2006/2012, Nicaragua 2005, Laos 2006, PNG 2005, Philippines 2011, Georgia
2009, Oman 2004, USA NHANES 2003–06). For any candidate already in BRINDA,
biomarker definitions and inflammation adjustment are already standardized.
Overview: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4785469/

### Access notes
- **GAIN / Nutrition International "Micronutrient Survey Toolkit"** is a
  **methods manual, NOT a microdata repository** — protocols only, no datasets.
- Microdata routes vary: **DHS Program** (Malawi MNS, Tanzania 2010, Uganda,
  Rwanda); **GHDx request** (Nigeria 2021, Sierra Leone, Gambia, Pakistan,
  Bangladesh, Ethiopia); **national stats office** (Kenya KNBS); **HSRC DUA**
  (South Africa); **direct PI request** (Cameroon).

## (3) MICS surveys — micronutrient biomarker modules

**Bottom line: MICS is NOT a source of serum micronutrient biomarkers.**
Standard MICS questionnaires collect no blood biomarkers — only household
indicators, a **household salt-iodine commodity test** (rapid kit, ≥15 ppm —
not urinary iodine), vitamin A **supplementation coverage** questions (not
serum retinol), child anthropometry, and (MICS6) water-quality E. coli testing.

- **No serum ferritin / RBP / retinol / zinc / folate / B12 or urinary iodine
  in any standard MICS round, including MICS6.**
- **Anemia/Hb:** only in some early rounds (MICS2 ~1999–2001, MICS3 ~2005–06,
  e.g. Georgia 2005, Moldova 2012) and as occasional country-specific add-ons —
  not a standard MICS4/5/6 module. For anemia, DHS is the standard source.
- **Exceptions:** the UNICEF MICS FAQ confirms biomarkers appear only as rare
  government-requested add-ons with partner (e.g. ICF) support — examples
  (Central African Republic, Ghana, São Tomé & Príncipe) are **anemia/Hb**
  add-ons, not serum micronutrient panels.

**Verdict:** Use MICS only for household covariates, salt-iodization-at-home,
vitamin A coverage, anthropometry, occasionally anemia — **not** serum
micronutrient biomarkers.

Sources: MICS FAQ https://mics.unicef.org/faq · MICS6/SCORE
https://score.tools.who.int/tools/survey-populations-and-health-risks/tool/multiple-indicator-cluster-survey-6-mics6-3/ ·

## Caveat relevant to this project's subnational resolution

No standalone survey here is powered below **admin-1 (region/province)**
**except Pakistan NNS 2018 (district-representative)** and SANHANES
(provincial). For all others, design strata are national + region —
consistent with the project's known issue that proxy predictors are
admin-2-level while biomarker surveys resolve to ~admin-1.
