# LV-02 and NZ-01: what the ranking adds to levels, and how much error is survey noise (28 Sep 2026)

Both designs were fixed before any result was seen. Scripts:
`scripts/protocol_v2/78_flat_national_comparator.R` (LV-02) and
`scripts/protocol_v2/79_prevalence_noise_split.R` (NZ-01). Script 66 (CP-01) was
rerun unchanged.

## LV-02: anchor plus ranking (A1) against the flat national anchor (A0)

A0 gives every district the same national anchor draw that A1 uses. Everything
else is the same: fraction f, units and scoring (district MAE in points).
Pre-registered reading: the ranking adds to level accuracy only where A1 beats
A0 on average AND in a majority of cells.

**Reproduction.** A1 here matches all 14,080 published A1 draws in
`anchor_and_rank.csv` (max difference 5e-14). The summary matches
`anchor_and_rank_summary.csv` exactly.

**Internal, 22 cells.** Each entry is the mean per-cell A1 minus A0 (points), with
the number of cells where A1 wins in brackets:

| ranking from / domains | f = 0.05 | f = 0.25 | f = 1.00 |
|---|---|---|---|
| level / climate + soil | -0.07 (11) | -0.13 (11) | -0.08 (11) |
| level / full | +0.14 (10) | +0.16 (10) | +0.16 (11) |
| prev / climate + soil | -0.16 (11) | -0.16 (11) | -0.16 (11) |
| prev / full | +0.12 (8) | +0.15 (8) | +0.15 (8) |

No configuration passes at any fraction. Climate + soil is better on average by
a tenth of a point, but it wins 11 of 22 cells, which is a tie and not a
majority. The published A1 figure of 9.60 at f = 0.05 against a median A0 of
10.11 looks like a gain, but those two medians come from different cells; the
median per-cell difference is -0.01. A1 wins where the ranking held up in the
held-out country (for example Gambia iron, -1.4 to -2.2) and loses where it did
not (for example Sierra Leone women's iron, +2.1).

**External, WHO VMNIS admin-1 prevalence, 29 cells.** The saved external
validation tables keep only per-cell Spearman, not a score for each unit. The
unit predictions were rebuilt with script 04's own code and inputs. All 29
Spearman values match `xv_transport.csv` to 1e-6.

| cells | mean A1 - A0 | A1 better / worse / tied |
|---|---|---|
| Africa, iSDA (21) | +0.01 | 7 / 8 / 6 |
| South Asia, SoilGrids (8) | +0.41 | 4 / 2 / 2 |
| all 29 | +0.12 | 11 / 10 / 8 |
| anchor from the deposit's national row (10) | +0.04 | 5 / 3 / 2 |

Result: fails the pre-registered reading in both arms. The 8 ties are the B12
and folate cells: their nested training rho is negative or zero, so it is
floored at 0 and A1 equals A0. The anchor was the deposit's own national row in
10 cells (Ethiopia 5, India 2, Pakistan child 2, Sudan child iron 1). No
population data exist on disk for these countries, so option 2 was never
available. The other 19 cells use the unweighted admin-1 mean (option 3). That
anchor is circular and favours A0. A secondary run was declared in advance: sd
at district rung, the Cote d'Ivoire figure's choice. It does worse (mean +1.38,
A1 better in 6 of 29).

**Reading.** Almost all of the level accuracy comes from the national figure. At
the shrinkage the training countries justify, the transported ranking neither
helps nor hurts district levels on average. Its value is in the ordering
(targeting), not in the level.

## CP-01 rerun on the fixed targets

The pre-fix outputs were confirmed byte-identical in
`mn-prediction-snapshots/pre_rebuild_2026-09-27` before the rerun.

- Median 90% half-width fell from **26.0 to 23.4 points** (27 cells).
- Leave-one-out coverage stayed at 0.90-0.93 (mean 0.911). Under this rule it
  is set almost entirely by n, so it cannot move much.
- The biggest moves were in vitamin A (the new rule) and iron. Ghana child
  vitamin A fell from 29.0 to 18.8. Malawi child vitamin A rose from 10.5 to
  15.8 and Malawi women's from 2.2 to 7.8. Gambia child iron rose from 36.7 to
  40.6.
- Selenium and iodine are unchanged, because their inputs were not rebuilt.

**Stale anchor.** Script 66 still anchors on
`results/tables/national_estimates_all.csv` (1 Sep), which predates the survey
fixes. In 8 cells that anchor is at least 3 points off the post-fix district
data. Examples: Gambia child iron 37.5 vs 62.7; Gambia women's iron 28.2 vs
45.1; Malawi child iron 10.3 vs 20.5; Malawi women's iron 8.1 vs 17.1; Malawi
B12 2.3 vs 10.9. In those cells the mean residual matches the gap almost point
for point. The rerun bands for iron and Malawi B12 are therefore still partly
pre-fix. They should narrow once the national file is rebuilt.

## NZ-01: share of the error that is the survey's own noise

Observed MSE = true-error MSE + sampling variance. The variance is
mean(p(1-p)/n_eff), with residuals from the rerun above (calibrated index,
in-fill). n_eff = district Kish n / (1 + (b - 1) rho), where b is respondents per
cluster in the district and rho is the intra-cluster correlation implied by the
national design effect. It carries both the weighting and the clustering.

| scope | noise share | RMSE vs survey | implied RMSE vs truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| pooled, all 27 cells (pre-specified headline) | 47% | 19.0 | 13.8 |
| pooled, 22 panel cells (no zinc, selenium, iodine) | 60% | 15.9 | 10.0 |
| median over cells | 65% | 14.9 | 7.5 |

Per cell, noise share: Gambia iron child 0.40 / women 0.24, vitamin A child
0.62 / women 0.65. Ghana iron 0.65 / 0.83, vitamin A 0.67 / 0.96, B12 0.52,
folate 0.44. Malawi iron 1.00* / 0.44, vitamin A 1.00 / 0.93, B12 0.52, folate
0.79, zinc 0.65 / 0.57, selenium 0.10 / 0.10, iodine 0.38. Sierra Leone iron
1.00* / 0.63, vitamin A 1.00* / 1.00*, B12 1.00*, folate 0.56. * marks the 5
cells where the noise estimate exceeds the observed MSE; the true error there
is floored at 0.

**Headline.** About 47% of the gap between the model and the survey's district
figure is the survey's own sampling noise. Against the true prevalence the
typical error is about 14 points rather than 19. For the 22 panel cells it is
about 10 points rather than 16.

A post hoc diagnostic, not part of the design: the per-cell mean offset,
mostly the stale anchor, is 9% of the pooled MSE (18% in the panel cells).
Without it the error against the truth would be about 12.6 points (7.4 for the
panel cells).

## Caveats

- The sampling variance uses plug-in p. p(1-p)/n_eff is biased low by a factor
  of (n_eff - 1)/n_eff, which is 25% at the Malawi median n_eff of about 4. It
  is also zero wherever a district's p is 0. So the noise share is probably
  understated.
- District design effects rest on one national intra-cluster correlation per
  country and outcome, so they are uncertain in both directions.
- RMSE is used throughout, not the median absolute error that CP-01 quotes.
- In the external check, 19 of 29 anchors are circular (option 3). Sierra
  Leone enters the nested rho with only 4-5 admin-1 units.
